# What I work on

## Now

Senior GPU Architect with over 7 years of experience in system architecture for ASIC, GPU, and SoC devices for both graphics and AI/ML workloads.

Currently architecting the secure boot of NVIDIA GPUs (Blackwell, Rubin, and next gens), coordinating across many hardware, firmware, and software teams to deliver a secure initialization of the system, from power-up to SW.

power-up→hardware→firmware→SW

## Before

I have also worked 5 years developing embedded solutions and hardware-software co-design projects for photonic devices at Lumentum/Idea.

Master in Computer Science by UNICAMP, with a specialization in Computer Architecture and Energy Efficiency.

Creator of ReonV (2018), one of the first RISC-V processors published from Brazil.
